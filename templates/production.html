How It Works
  1. Select an active order from the dropdown
  2. Enter completed and/or rejected quantities
  3. Total cannot exceed remaining quantity
  4. Status updates automatically
  5. Entry is recorded in Excel history
Formula
Remaining = Total − Completed − Rejected

Rejected items are tracked separately and never counted as completed.
